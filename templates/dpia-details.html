Overall Mitigation Measures

Technical Measures
Encryption at rest and in transit, role-based access control, authentication, audit logging, monitoring, and controlled access to analytics datasets.
Organizational Measures
Privacy policies, employee training, access reviews, processor oversight, and periodic privacy assessments.
Data Minimization
Only data necessary for the defined analytics purpose should be collected and processed.
Retention Controls
Defined retention periods with periodic review and deletion of data that is no longer required.
